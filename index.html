GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We’ve got answers.

How do I start a project? +

Start with a WhatsApp conversation. Tell us what you’re planning, your date, location and whatever you already know. We’ll take it from there.

How does the quotation work? +

We understand the scope first, then prepare a quotation around the requirements. If the scope changes, we discuss the change before proceeding.

Is an advance payment required? +

Yes. A 50% advance is required to confirm the project and begin work. The remaining 50% follows the milestone or completion terms agreed in the quotation.

Can you work with my budget? +

Absolutely. Tell us your budget upfront. We’ll explore what can be achieved within it and suggest the smartest way to use the available spend.

Do you handle events outside Chennai? +

Yes. Share the event location with us and we’ll work through the logistics, travel and production requirements as part of the planning.
